# Day 15 — NumPy | Interview

**Why NumPy?** Efficient arrays, vectorized arithmetic, broadcasting, and numerical operations underpin many data-processing libraries.

**Vectorization?** Applying operations to whole arrays instead of Python-level element loops; often improves performance and readability.

**What is broadcasting?** NumPy's rules allow compatible shapes to participate in operations without manually copying data.

**How handle NaN?** Use explicit masks and NaN-aware operations where appropriate; test output because NaN propagates in many calculations.

**NumPy vs Pandas?** NumPy focuses on arrays/numerical computation; Pandas adds labeled tabular structures and data-oriented operations.

Scenario: vectorized output differs from loop. Check dtype, shape, integer overflow/casting, NaN, and broadcasting dimensions.
